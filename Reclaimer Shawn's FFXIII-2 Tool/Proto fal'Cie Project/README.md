# Goal of Project
Make enemy monsters usable as allies. Especially Proto fal'Cie Adam and the other bosses. Currently most of the monsters in the list below can be edited into your inventory. There are some stipulations with this, though:
  * Paradigm Shifting in battle will cause a crash with ANY of these monsters
  * Some monsters may crash the game upon their model loading, entering the battle, or in other ways I haven't anticipated yet. This IS A RESEARCH PROJECT!
  * Monsters here do not have a Crystarium of their own. As a result, they will not appear in the Monster List unless you use my tool to "Force" them in.
  * Getting a pre-emptive strike results in the AI glitching out and not attacking.
  * Many of these monsters will either not attack at all or will attempt to but do nothing.

# How I'll Test
Use my save editor to Force these monsters into the game. From there, I will subject monsters to two tests:
  * COMMANDO TEST: Every monster in this list will be tested with the same setup. A Commando Gorgonopsid will be morphed into the Monster and be given Ruin, Attack, Ruinga, Blindside, and Powerchain.                         If the monster is able to successfully use ANY of these moves or able to do ANY attack, it will be considered to pass the test.
  * RAVAGER TEST: Every monster in this list will be tested with the same setup. A Ravager Gorgonopsid will be morphed into the Monster and be given Fire, Thunder, Ruin, Attack, and Ruinga. If the monster is able 
  to successfully use ANY of these moves or able to do ANY attack, it will be considered to pass the test.
  * EXTENDED TEST: This will mainly apply to monsters I actually care about, like the Boss Monsters, Cie'th, and some Unique Enemies. A Gorgonopsid will be morphed into the Monster and be given attacks that are specific 
  to that monster alone. Monster attacks appear to start with their ID, followed by the attack. EX: m740_ac000
