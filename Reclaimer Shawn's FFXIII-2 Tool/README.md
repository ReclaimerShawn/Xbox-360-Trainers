# Known Glitches
* Infinite Health will make all players and enemies immune to poison damage and wounding damage. This is done on purpose to simply the workings of Infinite Health. AKA: I'm lazy. 
* If you somehow get Gadot or Lebreau into the leader slot, the game will crash. Don't do that. This is due to how the game works and I cannot fix this.
* Selecting duplicate party members will cause only one party member to go into battle. Don't do that. This is due to how the game works and I cannot fix this.
* If it says the Ability Editor has failed to connect, simply close the window and click the Ability Editor again to see if it works. I doubt you'll come across this problem if you're able to connect with the main menu.
* The monster editor has a few ways to crash the game or cause unexpected behavior. Certain Monsters in the Force section may crash on model load or battle load. Paradigm Shifting with said monsters with cause your game to crash. Consider this a debug/testing option. Many Monsters in the Force section may also be unable to use moves or behave strangely in battle. I theorize this is because the AI is expecting moves it doesn't have, and therefore, does nothing. I will attempt to write monster specific moves to monsters to see if I cannot force their AI to work.

# Potential Glitches
* Disturbance of story events or potential save corruption. To allow the other characters in, flags related to the characters are changed. Make a backup of your save before you use this tool. I cannot confirm or deny this is a problem that exists.
